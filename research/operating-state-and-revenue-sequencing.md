## Findings

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The current constraint is not the ability to build, research, or produce collateral. It is converting those assets into a measured, founder-owned lead → conversation → proposal → cash loop.

### The operating pattern is implementation-heavy

The last several hours and days concentrated on landing-page implementation, Field Notes, decks, bilingual routes, benchmark work, repository reconciliation, and orchestration handoffs [source](../external-sources/chronicle-and-runtime-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). In the most recent seven-day GitHub window, default-branch activity narrowed to three repositories and 15 commits even though the 30-day count looked much larger [source](../external-sources/github-org-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). This suggests bursts of deep production work rather than a stable company-wide operating cadence.

The portfolio contains strong implementation evidence: deployed or staged products, sales collateral, private meeting experiences, benchmark artifacts, and technical proof [sources](../external-sources/github-org-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). However, the same evidence does not establish current replies, meetings, proposals, paid conversions, CAC, retention, or repeatability. The recorded 50-lead batch and 80 drafts are useful inputs, but they are not a current pipeline without live CRM stage and touch data [source](../external-sources/github-org-snapshot-2026-07-26.md).

### The portfolio needs asymmetric allocation

| Lane | Current role | Next proof | Near-term allocation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agentic Engineering agency | Immediate cash engine | One accepted proposal and prepaid engagement from a qualified recurring-workflow buyer | Primary |
| Multiempaques | Closest concrete agency buying situation | Complete free Blueprint after data sufficiency; deliver price 7–10 days later; start paid implementation only after acceptance | Primary account |
| Curia | Separate flagship vertical, entity, and raise | Additional paid pilot/design partner with verified scope, governance, and source-quality controls | Primary product lane |
| Ultimate Harness | AE delivery IP and governance offer | Five engineering-leader interviews and one paid onboarding/governance workshop | Validation quota |
| PriceGenius | B2B option | Eight seller interviews, two manual pricing audits, one paid pilot | Validation quota |
| Defade | Bounded B2C option | Restore funnel integrity, instrument upload-to-purchase, then seek five paid packs | Validation quota |
| Muta | Incubation option | Three strong customer commitments for a hosted control plane | No build before demand |
| Prism Arena / MemSWE | Proof and lead-generation assets | Three buyer-facing proof pieces with explicit Fit Check path and qualified-conversation measurement | Enablement |

This allocation follows the founder-approved separation between agency/platform and Curia [source](./founder-interview-round-4-2026-07-26.md). It does not require abandoning the other products; it gives each one a bounded evidence gate before more build investment.

### Multiempaques is a controlled exception, not a pricing reset

The private Multiempaques Sites project is already optimized for a short Aspel-SAE discovery session [source](../external-sources/sites-portfolio-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). The founder approved a free Blueprint for this first-client validation case, with the pricing window starting only when enough data has arrived and paid implementation beginning only after price acceptance [source](./founder-interview-round-4-2026-07-26.md). The standard wider-market ladder remains free Fit Check → US$4,000 Blueprint → bounded pilot → operations retainer as recorded in current collateral and Linear [sources](../external-sources/sites-portfolio-snapshot-2026-07-26.md).

### Linear has the right parent but stale and conflicting children

`LP-23` already defines the founder-led growth system and human approval boundary, while `LP-17` records Twenty as the confirmed-contact CRM mirror [source](../external-sources/linear-operating-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). Older Apollo and HubSpot issues would create a 500-lead scale-up and a second CRM before a smaller founder-led pilot has proved the workflow. The provisional operating choice is to make `LP-23` the canonical parent, gate list expansion behind a 50-account pilot, and treat Twenty as canonical unless the founders explicitly choose otherwise.

### A ledger should precede a dashboard application

The company needs one daily view for Lalo and Lucy, but a new application before data contracts exist would become another product to maintain. The first useful artifact is a founder operating ledger with these records:

| Record | Minimum fields | Source of truth |
|---|---|---|
| Account | company, ICP evidence, owner, source, next action, stage | CRM |
| Touch | account, channel, draft/approved/sent, timestamp, outcome | CRM / outreach system |
| Opportunity | offer, amount, probability, close date, decision gate | CRM |
| Workstream | project, owner, current outcome, blocker, next milestone | Linear |
| Evidence | claim, URL/path, observed date, confidence, allowed use | Wiki |
| Cash | invoiced, collected, committed delivery cost, runway inputs | Finance ledger |
| Agent run | mission, model, cost, result, accepted/rejected, artifact | Agent run ledger |

A later dashboard should read these systems rather than become another write authority. Gmail remains unavailable until OAuth is repaired, and Paperclip remains unavailable until remote Tailscale/SSH/API health returns [source](../external-sources/chronicle-and-runtime-snapshot-2026-07-26.md).

## Trade-offs

- Concentrating on agency plus Curia slows visible progress across the rest of the portfolio, but increases the chance of getting interpretable revenue and customer evidence.
- A free Multiempaques Blueprint sacrifices the normal discovery fee, but is bounded to one early-client exception and can generate a priced implementation opportunity.
- Keeping humans on every send reduces throughput, but protects reputation and creates labeled training/evaluation data.
- A ledger-first dashboard is less visually impressive than a new command-center app, but avoids inventing a second source of truth.

## Open questions

- What exact Multiempaques fields constitute “data sufficient” and start the 7–10-day clock?
- What current stage, last touch, reply, meeting, and owner exists for each of the June Twenty records?
- What is the minimum paid Curia pilot scope that does not overstate production readiness?
- Who owns the founder ledger each day, and which metrics must Lucy see versus Lalo?
- Which proof asset produces qualified conversations rather than impressions?

## Tentative recommendation

Run two founder-owned lanes for 30 days: Agentic Engineering agency cash generation led by Multiempaques and a separate Curia paid-pilot lane. Give Defade, PriceGenius, and Ultimate Harness small validation quotas with explicit stop conditions. Reconcile Linear and CRM truth before building a dashboard application or expanding to 500 leads.
