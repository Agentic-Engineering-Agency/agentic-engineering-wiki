## Question

Which company-structure, portfolio, revenue, and operating decisions did the founder approve on 2026-07-26, and which parts remain target state rather than completed fact?

## Evidence context

This record combines direct founder direction with the point-in-time [GitHub organization snapshot](../external-sources/github-org-snapshot-2026-07-26.md), [Sites portfolio snapshot](../external-sources/sites-portfolio-snapshot-2026-07-26.md), [Linear operating snapshot](../external-sources/linear-operating-snapshot-2026-07-26.md), and [Prism SceneSpec snapshot](../external-sources/prism-scenespec-snapshot-2026-07-26.md).

## Founder-approved decisions

### Entities and raises

- Agentic Engineering and Curia will pursue separate raises.
- Curia is intended to become its own Mexican legal entity structured as a SAPI.
- Agentic Engineering is intended to become a Delaware corporation through Stripe Atlas.
- The agency is more tightly connected to Ultimate Harness and Muta than to Curia.
- These are approved target-state decisions. Incorporation, tax registration, capitalization, contract migration, and legal completion remain unverified until documentary evidence is indexed.

### Platform and IP thesis

- Agentic Engineering is intended to own and use the IP for Ultimate Harness, PriceGenius, and Defade.
- Muta, Agentforge, and SpecSafe are potential additions to the AE platform/IP perimeter; their inclusion is not yet final.
- The strategic ambition is a multi-model agent platform analogous in category—not claimed parity—to Factory, Amp, Devin, Manus, OMO, OMP, and Sakana-style model ensembles.
- The desired outcome is task-specific routing and verification that can outperform any single constituent model on the workflows for which the harness is validated.
- No “better than every individual model” claim is approved until a task-specific sales, delivery, or engineering evaluation demonstrates it.
- Legal IP assignments, licenses, founder invention assignments, and repository ownership remain diligence gates.

### Portfolio and revenue sequencing

- Agency services are the immediate cash engine.
- Curia is a separate flagship vertical product, entity, and raise—not merely an agency feature.
- Ultimate Harness is delivery IP and a possible commercial governance/onboarding offer.
- PriceGenius should validate B2B demand through interviews, manual audits, and a paid pilot before more automation.
- Defade should be revived through a bounded B2C validation motion rather than a broad rebuild.
- Muta should be incubated behind customer commitments.
- Prism Arena and MemSWE should primarily function as proof and lead-generation assets until buyer evidence exists.
- No project is to be archived or deleted in the approved mutation batch.

### Multiempaques

- Multiempaques is in the process of supplying the operating data required for a Blueprint.
- The Blueprint is free for this engagement as an explicit early-client validation exception.
- A price should be delivered about 7–10 days after sufficient data is available to complete the Blueprint.
- Paid implementation starts only if Multiempaques accepts the proposed scope and price.
- The exception does not replace the standard paid Blueprint offer for the wider market.

### Operating boundary

- The approved first mutation batch is wiki and Linear only.
- No external sends, merges, deployments, access changes, archival, or deletion are authorized.
- Normal low-risk planning and documentation should continue without repeated founder confirmation.
- Fresh Claude and different-family review should be used for important validation, but neither reviewer substitutes for evidence or human approval of external actions.
- Big architecture, legal, financing, access, or irreversible decisions still require explicit founder direction.

## Decisions explicitly rejected

- A single blended Agentic Engineering/Curia raise.
- Treating Curia as merely an Agentic Engineering product line.
- Presenting all portfolio projects as equal near-term GTM priorities.
- Presenting the multi-model platform as already superior without task-specific evidence.
- Autonomous outreach or other external actions during this batch.

## Open questions

- Has either entity been filed, formed, capitalized, or issued tax identifiers?
- Which entity currently owns each repository, trademark, domain, customer agreement, model-harness asset, and founder-created invention?
- Will Muta, Agentforge, and SpecSafe enter the AE IP perimeter, and under what assignment or license?
- What financing instrument, amount, valuation/cap, and milestone set will each separate raise use?
- Which CRM is canonical after reconciling Twenty in the landing-page plan with older HubSpot work?
- What exact data-completeness gate starts the Multiempaques 7–10-day pricing window?
- Which 30-case and shadow-task results are sufficient to approve a model for production sales work?
