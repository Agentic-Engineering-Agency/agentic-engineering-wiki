What can Agentic Engineering and Curia truthfully say to customers and investors now, and which entity, financing, IP, customer, and economics gates remain before either raise is ready?

## Findings

### The raise architecture is decided; execution is not verified

The founder chose separate raises and separate target entities: Agentic Engineering as a Delaware corporation through Stripe Atlas and Curia as a Mexican SAPI [source](./founder-interview-round-4-2026-07-26.md). That decision resolves the narrative direction, but it does not prove either entity has been formed, capitalized, tax-registered, or made party to existing contracts.

The two inspected investor Sites projects each showed a US$750,000 ask while using different instrument language and omitting a clear AE↔Curia IP/contract relationship [source](../external-sources/sites-portfolio-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). Older Linear fundraising work also mixed entity, pricing, and financing decisions under a past-due issue [source](../external-sources/linear-operating-snapshot-2026-07-26.md).

### IP perimeter is a target-state policy

The founder intends Agentic Engineering to own Ultimate Harness, PriceGenius, and Defade IP, with Muta, Agentforge, and SpecSafe possible additions [source](./founder-interview-round-4-2026-07-26.md). Investor materials should describe this as the approved target portfolio until assignments, licenses, repository ownership, domains, trademarks, and customer contracts are documentary facts.

The defensible platform thesis is that agency delivery produces reusable orchestration, evaluation, and governance IP. The non-defensible current claim is that the router already outperforms every constituent model across general work.

### KLGV wording must be singular and conservative

The inspected Sites projects used three incompatible KLGV descriptions [source](../external-sources/sites-portfolio-snapshot-2026-07-26.md). The current approved wording is:

> KLGV Abogados is Curia’s single current paid design partner. Curia is deployed in pilot use, but is not yet a completed full-production or repeatable commercial deployment.

Do not use “production deployment,” “production customer,” or “repeatable deployment” until the acceptance evidence exists. Product deployment, paid design partnership, and production repeatability are different claims.

### Claims need explicit states

| State | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| Observed | Verified in a dated source or system | May be stated with scope and date |
| Founder decision | Approved target direction | State as “will,” “intends,” or “target,” not completed fact |
| Planned | Work is proposed but not approved or executed | Internal planning only |
| Unverified | Plausible but evidence is missing or stale | Do not publish; create an evidence task |
| Prohibited | Contradicted, misleading, sensitive, or unsupported | Remove from customer/investor material |

Every public claim should also carry an owner, evidence path, observed date, approved wording, approved audience, and review date.

### Diligence gates before outreach

1. Formation documents, tax identifiers, registered addresses, bank/payment ownership, and capitalization for each entity.
2. Founder invention, confidentiality, and IP assignments; product/repository/domain/trademark/license schedule.
3. Contract-counterparty map for KLGV, Multiempaques, Héctor Guerra, vendors, and future customers.
4. Separate financing instrument, amount, cap/valuation, use of funds, milestones, and cap table for AE and Curia.
5. Signed KLGV contract/payment and explicit permission for every public name, quote, metric, screenshot, and case-study element.
6. Reconciled customer, pricing, team-role, and pipeline language across Sites, decks, one-pagers, and Linear.
7. Instrumented delivery cost, gross contribution, conversion, CAC, retention, expansion, and churn definitions.
8. A claims review gate before any investor or customer-facing send.

## Trade-offs

- Separate entities and raises increase legal/accounting overhead, but make each investment thesis and IP boundary clearer.
- Conservative KLGV wording may feel less impressive, but reduces diligence and reputational risk.
- Delaying a broad platform-superiority claim sacrifices a headline, but allows Agentic Engineering to build task-specific proof that is harder to dismiss.
- A formal claims registry adds process, but prevents different decks from silently diverging.

## Open questions

- What has already been filed or signed, and where is the evidence indexed?
- Which current contracts must be assigned or novated after formation?
- Which entity funds and owns cross-company shared infrastructure?
- Are the AE and Curia US$750,000 figures still current, and what milestones does each fund?
- What customer evidence may be public, private-to-investor, or internal only?
- Who has final approval authority for claims: Lalo, Lucy, Fernando, counsel, or the named customer?

## Tentative recommendation

Adopt one claims registry immediately, update every customer/investor artifact to the conservative KLGV and target-entity language, and keep both raises in preparation—not active outreach—until entity, IP, cap-table, contract, and customer-evidence packets are indexed separately.
