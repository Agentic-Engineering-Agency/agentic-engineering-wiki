Internal and confidential. This replaces the expired July 16 launch plan.

## Objective

Turn controlled KLGV pilot use into trustworthy evidence of value and readiness, without calling the deployment full production or assuming referrals.

## Weekly cadence

1. **Workflow review:** inspect one repeated legal workflow, usage, exceptions, and manual fallbacks.
2. **Reliability triage:** rank product, data, privacy, notification, and support issues.
3. **Evidence capture:** update the pilot ledger with baseline, outcome, lawyer review, and incident data.
4. **Decision review:** choose the smallest next improvement and record owner/date.
5. **Commercial check:** ask for renewal, expansion, referral, or case-study signals only when supported and appropriate.

## Evidence checklist

- [ ] Named active pilot users and permissions
- [ ] Defined matter/workflow scope and data boundary
- [ ] Baseline manual process and time/risk measure
- [ ] Repeated weekly use, not one demonstration
- [ ] Verified legal-output review protocol
- [ ] Notification success/failure monitoring
- [ ] Incident, fallback, support, and resolution log
- [ ] KLGV acceptance of the pilot's current limitations
- [ ] Commercial next-step decision

## Production-readiness gate

Do not promote the pilot to production-ready until security/privacy review, reliable operation, backups/recovery, monitoring, support ownership, acceptance criteria, and contractual scope are documented and passed.

## Referral and case-study gate

A referral or case study requires KLGV approval, exact claims, anonymization/privacy review, and exclusion of confidential pricing, matter data, investment details, and unverified results.

## Escalation

Escalate immediately for missed legal notifications, unsafe data exposure, incorrect citations not caught by the review protocol, unavailable fallback, ambiguous contract scope, or related-party conflict.
