# Brand & domains

## Domain architecture

| Domain | Role | Audience | Language |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [agenticengineering.agency](https://agenticengineering.agency) | Corporate + **agency services** | Global prospects, partners | English-primary |
| [agenticengineering.online](https://agenticengineering.online) | **Curia product** marketing | Mexican law firms | Spanish-primary |
| GitHub [Agentic-Engineering-Agency](https://github.com/Agentic-Engineering-Agency/) | Open source + public engineering | Developers | English |
| LinkedIn [agentic-engineering-agency](https://www.linkedin.com/company/agentic-engineering-agency/) | Company presence | B2B network | EN + ES posts |

> [!NOTE]
> `.agency` sells **us as engineers**. `.online` sells **Curia as product**. Do not merge messaging — legal buyers on `.online` should not wade through generic agency services.

## Curia spinout (brand implication)

Curia will likely need its **own product brand** (potentially `curia.app` per public mockups) as a **separate entity** from Agentic Engineering agency.

Until spinout completes:
- Agency site may showcase Curia as flagship product (current state).
- Contracting and IP assignment must stay legible for both agency clients and Curia investors.
