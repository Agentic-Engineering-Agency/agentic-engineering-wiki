# Brand & domains

## Brand architecture

| Brand/surface | Intended role | Primary audience | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agentic Engineering | Services, multi-model platform, and selected AE product IP | Operators, technical teams, partners, AE investors | Do not present Curia equity or contracts as AE assets |
| Curia | Mexican legal-operations product | Law firms, legal partners, Curia investors | Separate SAPI and raise target |
| Agentic-Engineering-Agency GitHub org | Engineering and product repositories | Developers, diligence reviewers, collaborators | Repository presence does not prove IP ownership or production status |
| Product names | Defade, PriceGenius, Ultimate Harness, and candidates | Segment-specific buyers/users | Use only after naming, ownership, domain, and evidence checks |

Observed Sites projects include AE client sales, AE investor overview, Curia investor deck, Multiempaques Prep 30, and MemSWE results. Their observed access state was owner-only; do not describe them as public deployments.

## Domain rules

- AE service/platform messaging and Curia product messaging need separate conversion paths.
- Curia may be shown as a founder-built case study only with claims-approved status wording and without implying AE ownership.
- A shared infrastructure story is acceptable when the contractual/IP boundary is explicit.
- Do not change domains, access, or deployment state in this mutation batch.

## Product portfolio naming

Before promoting any portfolio brand, reconcile exact product name, repository, domain, trademark risk, entity owner, maturity, buyer, and canonical CTA. “Forgecraft,” “Agentforge,” and similarly named concepts must not be treated as the same asset without a founder decision and evidence.
