# Competitive landscape

## Agency services

### Threats (internal assessment)

| Threat | Why it matters | Our counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Big consultancies** adding AI practices (A) | Brand trust, procurement access | Spec+TDD discipline, faster slices, founder-led eng |
| **Productized legal AI** (Harvey-class) (C) | Collapses custom legal build SOWs | Push Curia product; agency does integration not rebuild |
| Freelance AI engineers | Price undercut | Production eval/observability story |
| Client internal teams + Copilot/Cursor | "We have AI already" | Audit finds agent gaps; operate phase |

### Where we win

- [Delivery process](./delivery-process.md) with **eval from commit one**
- [SpecSafe wedge](./open-source-strategy.md) — credible AI-TDD story
- **Dual ICP** — MX ops + global startup infra ([ideal client](./ideal-client-profile.md))

## Curia (Mexico legal)

### Primary competitor (founder view)

**C — Generic ChatGPT + spreadsheets + manual Outlook**

Not (yet) dedicated Mexican legal SaaS incumbents or Harvey in MX mid-market.

| Alternative | Weakness Curia exploits |
| --- | --- |
| Manual bulletin review | Miss risk, partner time sink |
| ChatGPT ad hoc | Hallucinated citations, no case context |
| Excel deadline tracking | No monitor → calendar chain |

### Secondary / emerging

- Incumbent legal software adding AI bolt-ons (watch list)
- US legal AI expanding to LATAM (long horizon)

### Positioning line (internal)

Curia sells **trust architecture** (verified citations, single ops chain), not "another AI chat for lawyers."
