# Account discovery evidence rules

## Admission gate

An account enters a research pilot only when public, permission-compatible sources establish:

- legal or trade identity and registrable domain;
- Mexican footprint, sector, operating model, and size band;
- one recurring workflow with direct evidence;
- one ERP, system, urgency, or change signal;
- an explicit disqualifier check;
- observed-at time, source URL, short supporting snippet, confidence, allowed use, reviewer, and human disposition.

Unknown is allowed. Inference is not.

## Identity grains

Keep source record, establishment, legal entity, and account/domain separate. Never merge different corporate RFCs automatically. A shared logo, trade name, or address is insufficient evidence of one account.

## Claim confidence

Score each claim independently: authority 0–3, directness 0–2, freshness 0–2, independent corroboration 0–2, and entity match 0–1. High is 8–10; Medium is 5–7; Low is 0–4.

Reject claims without an entity match or supported only by a search-result snippet. ERP, pain, and urgency require Medium or better.

## Safety boundary

Company-level research does not authorize named-person research, personal contact collection, CRM writes, messages, sends, deployments, access changes, archival, deletion, or expansion beyond the approved batch. Curia-fit legal prospects remain in Curia's separate pipeline.
