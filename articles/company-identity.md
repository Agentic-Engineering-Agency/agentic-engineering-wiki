# Company identity

Agentic Engineering is currently a founder-led agentic-engineering business: it sells focused implementation work, develops reusable multi-agent infrastructure, and builds a portfolio of product IP. Its target company form is a **Delaware corporation created through Stripe Atlas**; that is a target, not a completed filing.

## Positioning

**Practical promise:** design, build, evaluate, and operate consequential AI workflows with explicit evidence, human approval gates, and model-independent quality controls.

The company is evolving from a pure agency description toward a platform thesis: repeatable harnesses and multi-model routing that can deliver more consistent task-level quality than relying on a single model. This is an investment and product thesis, not a verified universal superiority claim.

## Operating layers

| Layer | Role | Truth boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Services | Near-term cash engine and customer discovery | Founder-led; capacity remains selective |
| Harness / platform | Reusable routing, evaluation, observability, and workflow infrastructure | Must pass task-specific evaluations before performance claims |
| Product portfolio | Owned or assignable IP that can become wedges into the platform | Chain of title and market demand must be documented |
| Proof assets | Prism, MemSWE, benchmarks, case studies, and delivery artifacts | Evidence, not substitute revenue |

## Relationship to Curia

Curia is strategically related but is **not a feature or division of the future AE corporation**. The approved target is:

- **Agentic Engineering:** separate Delaware corporation, separate raise, services + platform + selected product IP.
- **Curia:** separate Mexican SAPI, separate raise, legal-operations product and contracts.

Shared founders, infrastructure, or service arrangements must be documented at arm's length. See [[articles/legal-entity-structure|Legal entity structure]].

## Intended AE IP portfolio

The founder's current intent is for AE to own or receive valid assignments for:

- Ultimate Harness
- PriceGenius
- Defade
- potentially Muta, Agentforge, and/or SpecSafe

This list records intent. It does not establish present ownership, repository identity, assignment, or freedom to operate. Those require an asset schedule and signed chain-of-title evidence.

## Capacity and quality bar

- Keep delivery selective until throughput is measured; historical guidance was 1–2 concurrent client engagements.
- Automations may research, enrich, score, and draft. A human approves external sends, proposals, prices, contracts, and investor claims.
- A model's reputation never overrides evidence for the exact task and harness.
- Public copy must distinguish deployed pilots, paid customers, design partners, repeatability, and production readiness.
