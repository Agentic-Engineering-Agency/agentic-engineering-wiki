## Positioning (internal truth vs public copy)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Public copy claims "Global AI Engineering Agency" and "senior engineering review from day one." **Internal capacity reality:** we intentionally cap at **1–2 concurrent client engagements**. The global/senior framing is **aspirational marketing**, not a bench we staff today.

### What we actually sell that others struggle with

Our differentiated delivery stack combines:

1. **SpecSafe discipline** — spec-driven development with TDD/QA gates on AI-assisted work ([SpecSafe](../articles/open-source-strategy.md#specsafe) wedge; ambition TBD).
2. **Multi-agent orchestration with eval and observability from commit one** — not demo agents; production systems with monitoring, fallback paths, and human review gates.

See [delivery process](./delivery-process.md) for how this shows up in client work.

## Agency vs product (Curia)

**Curia is intended to become a separate entity (spinout).** The agency and the legal-AI product are strategically linked today (shared founders, KLGV design partnership) but **not permanently co-mingled**. Client services work should not be blocked by Curia, and Curia IP should not create ambiguity for non-legal agency clients.

Details: [Curia — product overview](./curia-overview.md), [Brand & domains](./brand-and-domains.md).

## Capacity & quality bar

- **Concurrent engagements:** 1–2 max (founder decision, 2026-06-26).
- **Quality principle:** "Quality doesn't explode into confetti" — we refuse volume that breaks spec/TDD discipline.
- **When to say no:** Engagements that need a full senior bench we don't have, or that conflict with Curia spinout timeline without explicit tradeoff approval.
