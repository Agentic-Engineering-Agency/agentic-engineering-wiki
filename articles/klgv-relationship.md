> [!WARNING]
> Internal only. Customer terms, usage, investor relationships, and individual identities require need-to-know handling.

## Canonical customer wording

**KLGV Abogados is Curia's single current paid design partner. Curia is deployed in pilot use, but is not yet a completed full-production or repeatable commercial deployment.**

Use this wording until supported evidence changes the claim state.

## What the relationship should produce

- prioritized real-world workflows
- weekly usage and reliability evidence
- lawyer-reviewed output quality
- documented product and support gaps
- quantified operational value where possible
- renewal, expansion, referral, or case-study decisions

## Commercial and investor boundaries

Historical internal notes describe discounted design-partner terms and an investor/client relationship. This batch did not audit executed commercial terms, a financing instrument, or a current cap table. Therefore:

- keep those details confidential
- do not state an investment or ownership position externally without executed evidence
- do not infer Curia or AE cap-table rights from the design-partner relationship
- route any related-party structure through founders and counsel

## Dependency risks

| Risk | Signal | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single design-partner concentration | Product decisions overfit one firm's practice | Recruit a second qualified firm and distinguish firm-specific from reusable needs |
| Pilot called production | Marketing language outruns reliability evidence | Enforce claims registry and readiness ladder |
| Confidentiality/privacy failure | Matter or personal data enters an unsafe workflow | Data inventory, permissions, minimization, audit trail, counsel review |
| Price anchoring | Early terms become assumed market price | Run explicit pricing interviews and document standard-package economics |
| Client/investor conflict | Commercial feedback affects financing or related-party decisions | Separate records, approvals, and counsel review |
